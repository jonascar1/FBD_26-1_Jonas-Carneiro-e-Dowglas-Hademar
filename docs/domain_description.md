# Domain description

The condominium management system project aims to manage important data related to residential condominiums and their residents, enabling data consultation, payment control, and the management of multiple housing units in a unified and integrated way within a single platform, ensuring simplicity and organizational efficiency.

For organizational purposes, individuals in the system are categorized as Residents or Employees, each with specific and distinct roles. Residents are linked to apartments, may be the primary occupant responsible for the unit and may also have dependents who live in the same unit. Employees, on the other hand, are linked to the condominium and have specific information such as role and salary. For example, a resident named Dowglas may be associated with Apartment 143 as its primary occupant, with his sister Maria registered as a dependent living in the same unit. Additionally, an employee named Jonas may be assigned to the condominium, working as an administrator and earning a salary of R$ 2,400,00. Each type of user has its own characteristics and data within the system, contributing to the proper organization of information.

The system addresses the difficulty of organizing and managing data in condominiums, which was previously done manually, making access to information less efficient and more limited, as well as more time-consuming to manage. By centralizing data, the system ensures greater integrity, consistency, and ease of access to information, enabling efficient management of relationships between residents, apartments, dependents and condominium fees. In this way, it reduces reliance on informal and bureaucratic processes, promoting a more organized, agile, and reliable management approach.

The system is intended for use in residential condominiums and can be used by administrators, property managers, or others responsible for condominium management. Its application is primarily aimed at small and medium-sized condominiums with multiple units, where there is a need to organize administrative information for each unit, integrated with financial management that ensures functionality and transparency. Therefore, the project provides a simplified alternative to the previous informality and outdated management practices in this field.
